On 2026-10-27, after video 6 (stacked kinetic hook block plus one word at a time in the body), the user said they didn't like how the captions came out and asked to "keep something simpler" for the next videos.

**Default now (simple):** short groups of 2–4 words, at most 1–2 lines, centered in the free band between face and object. Montserrat Bold/SemiBold at about 60px, white, with a soft dark shadow only. No box or pill ever. Discreet entrance (a quick fade, or none) and no per-word effects. Gold accent only on the trigger number (e.g. 222). Keep the headline simple too: one short line at the top during the hook, removed at the transition to the avatar.

**Why:** the user found the kinetic style too busy for the niche, and they had already rejected the caption box ([[auraly-video-edit-playbook]]).

**How to apply:** don't use stacked blocks, mixed font sizes or one-word captions unless the user asks for them again. Punch-ins at sentence turns can stay, but keep them subtle.
