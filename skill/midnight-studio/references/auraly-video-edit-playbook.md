User's creative direction for Auraly funnel videos (FB/IG Reels), given 2026-10-21. Funnel: viewer picks 3 of 7 cards → connection message → Auraly sales video (spiritual reading). The video prepares the action "choose your three cards". Language = language of the received audio (so far English).

**Emotional arc:** visual surprise → curiosity with context → identification → small real delivery → desire to choose → access the three cards.

**Hook (first 3–5s):** action already happening on frame 1; each cut adds one detail; tight tactile closes + tactile sound. Use only the part of a 6–8s generated hook that keeps action moving — cut dead waiting. Reveal avatar when reaction/first line raises interest. Hook must open a question the video develops.

**Headline:** one idea, 3–8 words, high contrast, explains why to watch the opening *right now* (question, choice, pattern). Leaves or changes function at hook→body transition. E.g. "WHICH THREE WOULD YOU CHOOSE?", "WHY DOES THIS PATTERN KEEP REPEATING?", "BEFORE YOU LOOK FOR ANOTHER SIGN…". No unbacked promises, no expired dates.

**Body (3 moves):** recognizable situation/question → real interpretation with value → what they'll explore by choosing 3 cards. First line after hook bridges scene to theme. Keep expressive pauses/breaths; cut repetitions and filler. One naturally shareable sentence. Stable framing, cuts at reasoning turns, subtle 5–10% punch-ins; inserts only show what speech mentions. No face-warping transitions, no clipped syllables, no aimless zoom.

**Captions:** keep them SIMPLE — see [[caption-style-kinetic-clean]] (kinetic style rejected 2026-10-27). All speech, manually checked. Montserrat SemiBold / Arial Bold, ~56–68px at 1080×1920. 2–5 word units, up to 2 lines. White text with soft dark shadow ONLY — never a box/pill/background behind captions (user finds it ugly, 2026-10-21); ONE accent color per piece (gold or violet) on the decisive word of SOME phrases only — not every word. Short discreet entrances, no per-word bounce. Sit in the free band between face and hands/object; avoid IG/FB UI zones. Headline, captions, CTA differ by role and position. Face and main object stay text-free.

**Symbols:** one trigger per video, spelled exactly as the automation (default 222; others 333, 444, 555, 888, 999, 11:11, "Amen", "I Claim", "I receive"). Subtle before, prominent at CTA. Never present numbers/cards as proof of results.

**Claims to avoid:** don't call the funnel a quiz, live 1:1, or scientific diagnosis; don't say the whole offer is free; no unsupported exclusivity, deadlines, financial results or personal knowledge of the viewer.

**Audio:** voice first, intelligible on phone speaker at low volume. Hook: emphasize gesture sounds. Instrumental light-tension/ambient bed that rises/falls with meaning and dips under CTA instructions. Reveal/bell SFX only on real turns. Target ≈ -16 to -14 LUFS integrated, ≤ -1 dBTP.

**Pre-click:** before the CTA, show the next step (choose 3 cards); real page capture only, never an invented result screen.

**CTA:** Facebook — say "Comment 222 and I'll send you the link to choose your three cards." On screen "COMMENT 222" / "Get the link by message." Instagram — "Tap my profile picture and open my Stories. The link to choose your three cards is there." On screen "CHOOSE YOUR THREE CARDS" / "Link in my Stories." No fake buttons. If only one file for both platforms → use the Stories ending.

**Length/export:** test 35–60s first. 9:16 MP4 H.264 + AAC, 1080×1920, keep the main footage frame rate, no artificial sharpening. Deliver files named per platform. Check with/without audio and on a phone-size view. Then clean up per [[video-cleanup-after-render]].

**Variants:** to test hooks keep body + CTA identical and change only the opening; test headline in a separate round.

**Avatars:** Ariel, Sean, Baldur, Clara, Astra, Zen — preserve identity, clothes, accessories, marks, environment; same recognizable editing language, intensity adapted per avatar.

**Scope of my job:** the user sends videos already produced (hook clip + avatar with final script/voice). I only EDIT: trim dead waiting/silences, cut order, headline, captions, on-screen CTA text, music/SFX, mix, export. I do not rewrite the script, regenerate voice, or change what the avatar says; script-level rules above (body moves, spoken CTA, claims) guide how I edit and what text I add on screen. If the spoken copy conflicts with a rule (e.g. two triggers, deadlines), keep it and flag it briefly to the user.

**How to apply:** Use this as the default brief for any Auraly video edit with HyperFrames; ask only what the footage doesn't answer (platform → which CTA, trigger, theme). Tooling notes: [[hyperframes-setup-on-m-drive]].
